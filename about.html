About

Football. Properly Written.

JustStepover is independent football journalism for people who care about the game beyond the highlight reel.

We started JustStepover because football deserves writing that matches its drama — tactical depth, cultural context, and opinion that earns its place. No clickbait, no filler, no recycled press-conference quotes dressed up as analysis.

What we cover

From the Premier League title race to Champions League nights, transfer windows, tactical breakdowns, fan culture, and the stories that sit at the edge of the pitch — we write for readers who watch the whole match, not just the goals.
